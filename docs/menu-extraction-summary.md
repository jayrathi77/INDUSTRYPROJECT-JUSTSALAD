## Ambiguities preserved

- **Questionable source spellings:** “PANNER / TOFU VEGGIES SALAD,” “PANNER TIKKA WITH VEGGIES,” and “BELGIUM CHOCOLATE …” appear in the PDF. They are preserved exactly in the menu data and should be confirmed before final publication.
- **Sandwich bread prices:** The page lists “MULTIGRAIN BREAD ₹250 | SOURDOUGH BREAD ₹350” above five sandwiches. It gives no individual prices for the first four sandwiches, and separately prices “GUACAMOLE WITH SOURDOUGH | ₹399.” The bread prices may be bread choices or category pricing; they are recorded as printed and are not silently applied to the first four items.
- **Herbed Rice With Creamy Gravy:** The listed price is ₹299. The phrase “choice of creamy paneer or mixed vegetable gravy” is retained as the option; no option price is stated.
- **Paneer Tikka With Veggies** and **Thecha Paneer With Veggies:** Both list “quinoa or brown rice,” with no variation pricing. The source text is retained without assigning a surcharge.
- **Nutrition:** Approximate kcal, protein, carbs, fat, and fiber are printed for the six salads only. No nutrition data is supplied for other items.
- No availability status, Swiggy listing URL, delivery fee, tax, or payment configuration appears in the PDF.
